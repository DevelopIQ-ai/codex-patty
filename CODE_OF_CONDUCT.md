# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to Patty to be worth your time regardless of who you are. Everyone taking part — issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions — is expected to help keep it a harassment-free experience.

## Expected

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't, and say what you actually mean.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a feature. Patty is deliberately small.
- Respect the security rules: never post a `cp_live_…` key, a provider key, or an `auth.json` in a public issue, and never quote someone else's.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks; sexualised language or attention; publishing others' private information; deliberately derailing discussions; and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report to **kush@puffle.ai**, privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond within a week. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban an account for repeated or severe violations. Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith face the same consequences.
